Introduction

Tiger Palace by Soaltee is a luxury 5-star resort and casino located in Tilottama, Bhairahawa, Nepal, spanning across a sprawling 22-acre property. It is a premier destination in the Terai region, popular for leisure getaways, destination weddings, and conferences due to its proximity to the UNESCO World Heritage site of Lumbini.
