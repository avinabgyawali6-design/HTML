About TIGER PALACE

Tiger Palace by Soaltee is a luxury 5-star integrated resort and entertainment destination located in Bhairahawa (Tilottama-13), situated in the fertile Terai region of southern Nepal. Spanning over 22 acres of landscaped greenery, the property is highly popular for leisure getaways, destination weddings, and corporate conferences due to its strategic proximity to the India-Nepal border (Sunauli) and the UNESCO World Heritage site of Lumbini.
